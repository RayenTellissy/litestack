**Fixed.** `python3 -m tabsplit 100.00 alice bob carol` now prints `Total: $100.00`. Before, if a bill did not divide evenly, the shares added up to less than the bill. Up to one cent per person went missing. If a name was typed twice, a whole share went missing.

**What was broken.** Two separate bugs made the Total come out lower than the amount entered.
- **Uneven splits.** $100.00 over three people gave each person $33.33, for a total of $99.99. `80.00 alice bob carol --tip 15` totalled $91.98 instead of $92.00 (measured).
- **Repeated names.** `90.00 bob bob carol` printed one `bob` line and `Total: $60.00`. I found this while testing the "always" in your request. It already happens on `main` (measured).

**Root cause.**
- `allocate` in `tabsplit/money.py` computed `total_cents // parts` and gave that share to everyone, so the remainder was dropped. A probe printed `allocate(10000, 3) = [3333, 3333, 3333] sum = 9999` (measured).
- `split_even` in `tabsplit/bill.py` stored the shares in a dict keyed by name. A second `bob` overwrote the first one's share.

**Fix.**
- `allocate` now uses `divmod` and gives the leftover cents one each to the first people. Shares still differ by at most one cent.
- `split_even` now returns `(name, amount)` pairs in the order the names were typed. `receipt` adds up those pairs.

**Needs your call.**
- **Who pays the extra cent.** It goes to the people typed first, so alice pays $33.34. It could go to the last person instead, or rotate.
- **Repeated names.** Each one gets its own line and share, so `bob` prints twice. The alternatives are to merge them into one `bob: $60.00` line or to reject repeated names with an error.
- **Amounts that are read wrong.** Some amounts still don't match because `parse_amount` misreads them:
  - `-1.50` totals `-$0.50`.
  - `-0.50` totals `$0.50`.
  - `10.005` is cut to `$10.00`.

  `main` does the same. I left it out because the problem is in parsing the amount, not in splitting it, and the split now keeps whatever amount it gets. Fixing it needs a choice: accept negative and sub-cent amounts, or reject them when they are entered.
