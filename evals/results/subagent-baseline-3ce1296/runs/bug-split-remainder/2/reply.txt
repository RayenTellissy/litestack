Splitting a bill now keeps every cent. `python3 -m tabsplit 100.00 alice bob carol` prints `Total: $100.00`. The first person listed pays the extra cent ($33.34), and everyone else pays $33.33.

**What was broken.** When the amount didn't divide evenly between the people, the leftover cents vanished. The printed total came out short of the amount entered (plus tip).

**Root cause.** `allocate` in `tabsplit/money.py` used floor division and threw away `total % parts`. I probed each stage of the path. `parse_amount("100.00")` returns 10000 and `format_amount` is correct. `allocate(10000, 3)` returned `[3333, 3333, 3333]`, which sums to 9999. That is where the cent was lost. It is not a regression, because the repo has only the initial import commit.

**Fix.** A two-line change in `allocate`. It uses `divmod` and gives one extra cent to each of the first `leftover` shares. Even splits come out the same as before. The receipt still adds up the shares to print the total, so a share that goes missing still shows up as a wrong total.
