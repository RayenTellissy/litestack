The repro is fixed. `python3 -m tabsplit 100.00 alice bob carol` now prints `Total: $100.00`. But "always" still fails for three other inputs. Each goes through separate code that this change does not touch, and each fails the same way on `main`:

- `python3 -m tabsplit 90.00 alice alice bob` prints `Total: $60.00`.
- `python3 -m tabsplit -- -5.50 alice` prints `Total: -$4.50`.
- `python3 -m tabsplit 10.005 alice` prints `Total: $10.00`.

All three are under Needs your call below, with what fixing each would take.

## What was broken

Anyone who split a bill that didn't divide evenly in cents saw a total short by up to a cent per person, minus one. The money was lost before the receipt was printed. Nothing was wrong with how it was shown.

## Root cause

`allocate` in `tabsplit/money.py` used floor division (`total_cents // parts`) and gave every person that same share, so the leftover cents were dropped. I ran each stage separately to find it. `parse_amount('100.00')` returned 10000 and the tip step added nothing. `allocate(10000, 3)` returned `[3333, 3333, 3333]`, which adds up to 9999. The bug has been there since the first commit (`git log -L` on those lines shows only `51abbe1`).

## Fix

`allocate` now uses `divmod`. The leftover cents go one each to the first people listed. Shares always add up to the amount and never differ by more than one cent. This also fixes negative totals, which used to lose cents the same way. `-1.00` split three ways printed `-$1.02` and now prints `-$1.00`. `split_even` is the only caller. Two lines changed.

### Needs your call

1. **Who pays the extra cent.** The people listed first pay it now (alice $33.34, bob and carol $33.33). With a negative total, the last people listed get the larger negative share. Giving it to the last person, rotating it or choosing at random would also have worked.
2. **A name typed twice.** `90.00 alice alice bob` prints `Total: $60.00`. `split_even` stores shares by name, so the second "alice" overwrites the first. Fixing it needs a choice: reject repeated names at the command line, or keep one line per entry.
3. **Negative amount with cents.** `-- -5.50 alice` prints `Total: -$4.50`. `parse_amount` adds the cents as positive even when the dollars are negative. Fixing it means applying the sign to the whole amount, or rejecting negative amounts.
4. **More than two decimals.** `10.005 alice` prints `Total: $10.00`. `parse_amount` silently drops digits past the second decimal. Fixing it means choosing whether to reject the input or round it.

With `--tip`, the Total is still the amount plus the tip, as before. I read "the amount entered" as the amount being split.

## Principles that changed a decision

- **Fix root causes.** I fixed `allocate`, where the cent is dropped. I did not touch the Total line in `receipt`. Printing the entered amount there would have hidden shares that don't add up.
- **Smallest change.** I left the repeated-name and parsing bugs out. Each has its own cause and needs a product choice.
- **Test behavior.** The tests check the printed Total line and that shares add up and stay within a cent. They don't check how `allocate` works inside, so they don't fix who gets the extra cent.
- **Verifiable units.** The commits run in order: failing tests marked as expected failures first, then the fix, which removes the marks.
